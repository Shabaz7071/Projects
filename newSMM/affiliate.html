Who We're Looking For

Requirements & Toolkit

We aren't looking for the biggest accounts — we're looking for the most authentic. If you genuinely align with what VARRO stands for, you'll thrive as a partner.

Active Public Profile

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or a personal blog with regular posts. We're platform-agnostic — quality over quantity.

Men's Fashion or Lifestyle Niche

Your content should broadly relate to menswear, style, grooming, or lifestyle. Sustainable fashion and quality-focused audiences convert especially well.

Genuine Brand Alignment

You don't need to own VARRO pieces to apply (we can arrange that), but your aesthetic and values should align with ours: purposeful, elevated, quality-first.

No Minimum Follower Count

A micro-influencer with 3,000 engaged followers can outperform a passive 200K account. We evaluate engagement and fit — not raw numbers.
